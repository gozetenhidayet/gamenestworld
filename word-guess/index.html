About Word Guess

Word Guess is our take on the daily word-guessing format that made Wordle a household name: figure out a secret five-letter word using green and yellow clues to narrow things down. Unlike the original, you can play as many rounds as you like -- and unlike a fixed six-try format, your guess limit here actually shifts with your level, tightening from 6 tries down toward a 3-guess minimum as you climb higher.

How to Play Word Guess

Guess the 5-letter word before you run out of tries (6 at low levels, fewer at higher ones). Green = correct letter, correct spot. Yellow = correct letter, wrong spot.

Word Guess Controls

🖱️ Click letters or ⌨️ Keyboard

Rules & Feedback

Guess the hidden 5-letter word. After each guess, letters turn green (correct letter, correct spot), yellow (correct letter, wrong spot), or gray (not in the word) -- the same feedback system Wordle popularized.

Difficulty Scales Two Ways At Once

The word list gets harder as your level rises -- levels 1-10 draw from common, everyday words, 11-20 mix in trickier ones, and past level 20 it's trickier words only. At the same time your guess limit shrinks: you start with the classic 6 tries, dropping toward a 3-guess minimum at higher levels, so it's not just the words getting harder, it's your margin for error getting smaller too.

Controls

Type on the on-screen keyboard or your physical one -- Enter to submit a guess, Backspace to delete a letter.

Strategy

Open with a word using common, distinct letters to gather the most information from a single guess. When a letter turns yellow, try it in a different position next guess rather than leaving it where it was -- it's confirmed to be in the word, just not there.

Common Mistakes

Repeating a gray (already-ruled-out) letter in a later guess wastes a try. Ignoring a yellow tile instead of relocating that letter is the other common way a solvable word slips away.

Scoring

Solving the word is worth a flat 100 points toward your level here, regardless of how many of your guesses it took -- there's no bonus for solving it faster, so use every guess you're given if you need to.

History

This format is modeled on Wordle, created by Josh Wardle and released publicly in October 2021, which was acquired by The New York Times in 2022 after becoming a global daily-puzzle phenomenon.

Tips for Word Guess

  • Open with common, high-frequency letters (E, A, R, S, T) in Word Guess to narrow things down faster.
  • In Word Guess, look for prefixes and suffixes you recognize -- they often reveal a word faster than reading it letter by letter.
  • Don't rush your first move in Word Guess; a slightly slower, more deliberate start usually saves guesses later on.

Frequently Asked Questions About Word Guess

No -- this version awards a flat 100 points for solving the word at all, regardless of how many of your guesses you used, so there's no extra reward for speed-solving.

Does Word Guess get harder the more I play?

Yes, on two fronts -- the word list shifts toward trickier words as your level rises, and your guess limit also shrinks from the classic 6 tries toward a 3-guess minimum.
