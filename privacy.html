Privacy notice

Updated November 6, 2026.

Local game data

Settings, favorites, levels and scores may be saved in localStorage on this browser. They are not synchronized to an account. You can remove them through your browser's site-data controls; this also removes saved progress.

Network requests

The hosting provider receives requests needed to serve pages, scripts and images, which can include your IP address and browser information in its logs. Pages may request fonts from Google Fonts. Those requests are handled by the respective service providers.

Advertising and analytics

This version does not load advertising or analytics scripts. If advertising or analytics is enabled later, this notice and the site's consent controls must be updated to describe the actual services and choices before those services run.

Contact and public reports

The contact page links to the project's GitHub repository. GitHub is a separate service with its own privacy policy. Do not post passwords, personal documents or other private information in public reports. No message is sent merely by visiting our contact page.

Children and personal information

The games do not require a profile or personal details. Please do not include children's personal information in public reports.

Privacy requests

Use the channels listed on the contact page. For data held by the hosting provider or GitHub, their own privacy controls and policies also apply.
