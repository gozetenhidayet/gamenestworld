Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information GameNest World ("we", "us", "the site") collects when you visit gamenestworld.com, how it's used, and the choices you have. We've tried to write this in plain language rather than dense legal boilerplate.

1. Information We Don't Collect

GameNest World does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information to play any game on the site.

2. Local Storage (On Your Device)

Your XP, level, streaks, favorites, best scores, and in-progress game state are saved using your browser's local storage — a small amount of data kept on your own device, not on our servers. This data never leaves your browser and is not linked to your identity. Clearing your browser's site data, using a different browser, or switching devices will reset this progress.

3. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns (for example, how many people visit, which games are popular, and which countries visitors come from). We use Google AdSense to display advertising that keeps the site free. Both Google Analytics and Google AdSense may set cookies or use similar technologies (such as device identifiers) in your browser to measure usage and, in the case of AdSense, to personalize the ads you see based on your browsing activity.

Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through these services. You can learn more about how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can control ad personalization for your Google account at adssettings.google.com. If you're in the European Economic Area or UK, ads may be served under Google's consent management framework, and you'll be asked to make choices about cookies and personalization the first time you visit.

4. Affiliate Links

Some pages include affiliate links to third-party products or services (for example, chess or brain-training apps). If you click through and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only link to products related to the categories of games on the site, and clicking an affiliate link takes you to the third party's own site, which has its own privacy policy.

5. Children's Privacy

GameNest World is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the site does not use accounts, no personal information is collected from any visitor, regardless of age. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us information in some other way (for example, through the Contact form), please reach out and we'll address it promptly.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA and Similar Laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, or to object to certain processing. Because we don't collect account-linked personal data directly, most requests will relate to advertising cookies — which you can manage directly through your browser settings or the Google Ad Settings link above. For any other privacy request, contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the site, its features, or applicable laws change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be reflected here directly.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or a privacy request? Reach us through the Contact page.
