Rules and controls

Swipe or press arrows to merge tiles — reach the 2048 tile!

Controls: ← → ↑ ↓ Arrow keys

Strategy and details for this version

Keep the largest tile near a corner and build a descending row alongside it. Try to leave empty cells for the next spawn. A tile merges at most once during a move; random new tiles mean that a promising position is not a guaranteed win.

Starting, restarting and progress

Play opens this game in the main site's game panel. Use Restart game to begin a fresh attempt, or Back to return to the catalogue. Saved levels and scores, when supported by this game, stay in this browser; they do not sync to another device. Clearing site storage removes that progress.

If something goes wrong

Close the panel and reopen the game first. If the problem persists, include the game name, device, browser and the action that caused it in a bug report. Sound may require a click before the browser allows playback.
