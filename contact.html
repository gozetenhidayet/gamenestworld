Contact Us

Last updated: August 19, 2026

We read every message that comes in — whether it's a bug report, a game idea, a privacy question, or general feedback.

Email

The fastest way to reach us is by email at contact@gamenestworld.com. We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.

What to Include

  • Bug reports: the game name, your device/browser, and what happened (a screenshot helps a lot).
  • Game suggestions: what the game is, and any site you've seen a similar version on, so we can understand the format quickly.
  • Privacy or data requests: mention "Privacy Request" in your subject line so it reaches the right place first.
  • Advertising or partnership inquiries: please include your company name and a short description of what you're proposing.

Before You Write In

If a game isn't loading, a hard refresh (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + R) resolves most one-off issues, since it clears a stale cached copy of the page. Check our FAQ section on the homepage too — it covers the most common questions about progress saving, mobile support, and ads.
