About Hangman

Hangman is the pencil-and-paper classic rebuilt for the browser: guess letters to reveal a hidden word before your attempts run out. The word list spans everyday vocabulary so it suits most ages.

How to Play Hangman

Click letters to guess the hidden word before the man is hanged.

Hangman Controls

🖱️ Click letters

Rules

A hidden word is represented by blank spaces. Guess letters one at a time; correct guesses fill in every matching blank, wrong guesses add a piece to the drawing and use up one of your limited wrong guesses.

Difficulty Scales With Level

The word list has 6 difficulty tiers tied to your level, from short everyday words like CAT and DOG at the start up to long, uncommon words like JUXTAPOSITION at the highest tier. Your wrong-guess allowance also shrinks as you level up -- starting around 6-7 wrong guesses and tightening toward a 3-guess minimum.

Controls

Click or tap a letter on the on-screen A-Z keyboard -- each letter can only be guessed once.

Strategy

Guess common vowels and high-frequency consonants (E, A, R, S, T, N) first to reveal the word's shape, then use the revealed letter pattern to narrow down likely whole words before spending your remaining guesses.

Common Mistakes

Guessing uncommon letters early, before the word's basic shape is visible, wastes guesses that get more costly at higher levels where you're given fewer of them to begin with.

History

Hangman is a centuries-old pencil-and-paper word game of uncertain exact origin, widely believed to have taken roughly its modern form in Victorian-era England before becoming one of the most common classroom and campfire word games worldwide.

Tips for Hangman

  • Open with common, high-frequency letters (E, A, R, S, T) in Hangman to narrow things down faster.
  • In Hangman, look for prefixes and suffixes you recognize -- they often reveal a word faster than reading it letter by letter.
  • Don't rush your first move in Hangman; a slightly slower, more deliberate start usually saves guesses later on.
