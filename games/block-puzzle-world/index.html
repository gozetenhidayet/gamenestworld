Block Puzzle World Controls

🖱️ Click & Drag

Real Drag & Drop

Pick up a piece with your finger or mouse and it lifts and follows your pointer, offset upward so it doesn't get hidden under your finger. As you move it over the board, the cells it would occupy glow green if the placement is legal or red if it isn't, before you ever let go.

Scoring & Combos

Placing a piece scores a point per cell; clearing a row or column adds a bonus on top. Clear more than one line in the same move and you get an escalating "Nice! / Great! / Amazing! / Perfect!" label, and clearing lines on back-to-back placements builds a combo multiplier that pays out more the longer the streak runs.

Tray Rules

You're dealt 3 pieces at a time, and a fresh set of 3 is only dealt once all three current ones have been placed -- not one at a time, matching how real Block Blast-style games deal pieces. The game also checks the board before dealing a new set so you're not handed an immediately unplayable trio, and leans toward smaller pieces once the board is more than about 55% full.

Controls

Press and drag a piece from the tray onto the 8×8 board with your finger or mouse, then release to drop it.

Strategy

Keep at least one full row or column nearly clear at all times as a release valve -- filling the board evenly without ever completing a line is the most common way a board gets stuck.

Common Mistakes

Placing large pieces in corners early removes flexibility you'll want later for oddly-shaped pieces. Ignoring the green/red preview and dropping on a hunch, rather than actually checking the glow before releasing, causes avoidable wasted placements.

High-Score Tips

Chasing multi-line clears in the same move is worth meaningfully more than the same number of cells cleared across separate single-line moves, thanks to the combo multiplier -- it's often worth waiting one extra placement to set up a double clear.

Tips for Block Puzzle World
