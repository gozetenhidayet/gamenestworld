About Crossword

Crossword is a compact mini crossword -- a 5×5 grid built from a few intersecting words, each with its own numbered clue. Type your answers into the open cells so every word matches its clue, then check your work; it's a quicker fix than a full newspaper-style crossword without losing the satisfaction of filling in the last answer.

How to Play Crossword

Click a cell and type letters to fill in the words crossing at that cell, using the numbered clues as guides. Press Check Answers when you're done.

Puzzle Variety

This mini crossword cycles through a small rotating set of 5×5 grids, each built from 3 intersecting words -- it's a quick, compact format rather than a full newspaper-style crossword with dozens of clues.

Controls

Click or tap a cell and type a letter; the grid accepts one character per cell.

Strategy

Solve the shortest, most confident word first, since its letters at intersection points narrow down the possibilities for the words crossing it.

Common Mistakes

Guessing a long word from the clue alone without checking it against the letters already confirmed at its intersections is the most common way to end up needing to backtrack.

History

The crossword format was invented by journalist Arthur Wynne and first published in the New York World newspaper in 1913 -- it's remained one of the most widely syndicated puzzle formats in the world ever since.

Tips for Crossword
