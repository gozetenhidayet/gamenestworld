Word Chain is a vocabulary-building game where each new word must start with the last letter of the one before it. The longer your streak, the more it tests how many different words you can pull from memory on the fly.

Tips for Word Chain

  • Open with common, high-frequency letters (E, A, R, S, T) in Word Chain to narrow things down faster.
  • In Word Chain, look for prefixes and suffixes you recognize -- they often reveal a word faster than reading it letter by letter.
  • Don't rush your first move in Word Chain; a slightly slower, more deliberate start usually saves guesses later on.
