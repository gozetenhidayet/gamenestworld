About Breakout

Breakout is the arcade classic where you move a paddle to keep a ball in play and chip away at a wall of bricks above. Timing your paddle position becomes second nature the longer you play.

How to Play Breakout

Move the paddle to bounce the ball and break all bricks.

Breakout Controls

🖱️ Mouse or ← → keys

Rules

A wall of 40 bricks (8 columns × 5 rows) sits above your paddle. Bounce the ball into bricks to clear them; let the ball fall past your paddle 3 times and it's game over. Clear every brick to win the round.

Scoring

Each brick destroyed is worth 10 points, and your best score is tracked so you can beat it on a later run.

Paddle Angle Control

Where the ball hits your paddle changes its bounce angle -- striking it near the edge sends the ball off at a much sharper angle than hitting it dead center. This is the main tool you have for aiming at a specific brick instead of just keeping the ball alive.

Controls

Move your mouse to follow the paddle, or use the left/right arrow keys for smaller, precise steps.

Strategy

Use edge hits deliberately to redirect the ball toward bricks in the corners of the wall, which get more awkward to reach once the middle rows are cleared and the ball's rebound angles change.

Common Mistakes

Only moving the paddle reactively to keep the ball alive, without ever using hit position to aim, misses the game's main skill mechanic. Standing still in the center returns most hits straight back into the same few bricks instead of spreading out the clear.

History

Breakout was created in 1976 at Atari -- with early hardware design credited to Steve Wozniak, working alongside Steve Jobs on the project -- as a single-player evolution of Pong, and its brick-breaking format went on to inspire decades of paddle-and-ball games.

Frequently Asked Questions About Breakout

Does it matter where the ball hits my paddle in Breakout?

Yes -- hitting the ball near the edge of your paddle sends it off at a sharper angle than a center hit, so you can use paddle position to aim at specific bricks, not just keep the ball alive.

How many lives do I get in Breakout?

Three -- the ball can fall past your paddle twice without ending the round, and a third miss ends it.
