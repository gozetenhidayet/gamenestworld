About Tetris Lite

Tetris Lite is a streamlined take on the falling-block classic: rotate and place each piece to clear full lines before the stack reaches the top. Speed increases the longer you survive, so quick decisions matter.

How to Play Tetris Lite

Tetris Lite Controls

← → move, ↑ rotate, ↓ faster, P pause

Scoring

Clearing lines is worth disproportionately more the more you clear at once: 100 points for a single line, 300 for two, 500 for three, and 800 for a full four-line Tetris -- each multiplied by your current level. A hard drop also adds 2 points for every row it falls through.

Pieces & Rotation

All 7 classic tetromino shapes are dealt randomly -- there's no 7-bag randomizer here, so in principle the same piece can come up several times in a row. Rotating near a wall or another block nudges the piece one column over automatically if that makes the rotation fit, a simplified version of modern Tetris's wall-kick system rather than the full SRS ruleset.

Speed & Levels

Your level rises every 10 lines cleared, and each level shortens the drop timer (500ms minus 40ms per level, down to a 50ms floor) -- by around level 11, pieces are already falling as fast as the game allows.

Controls

Arrow keys to move and soft-drop, up to rotate, X for an instant hard drop, P to pause -- plus on-screen buttons for touch.

Strategy

Keep the stack flat and leave one column open for the straight 4-long piece, since a saved-up four-line clear is worth far more (level × 800) than four separate single-line clears at the same level.

Common Mistakes

Assuming every rotation near a wall will succeed -- without full SRS kicks, some rotations that would work in other Tetris games simply won't fit here. Waiting too long to decide once the drop timer bottoms out at 50ms is the other common way a stack tops out.

History

Tetris was created in 1984 by Soviet software engineer Alexey Pajitnov and went on to become one of the best-selling video games ever made -- the entire falling-block genre is still named after it.

Frequently Asked Questions About Tetris Lite

Does Tetris Lite use the official SRS rotation system?

Not the full system -- rotating near a wall or block nudges the piece one column over if that makes it fit, which covers most cases but isn't the complete Super Rotation System competitive Tetris uses.

Is there a hold-piece slot in Tetris Lite?

Not currently -- there's a Next piece preview, but no hold-piece slot to store a piece for later.
